About Me

I am an ELLIS-funded postdoctoral researcher at the University of Vaasa and Aalto University, Finland.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, wireless communications, control systems, and intelligent autonomous systems. My current research focuses on semantic communication and control co-design, particularly developing machine-learning-based approaches for efficient communication, prediction, decision-making, and control in connected and autonomous systems.

My research interests include predictive representation learning and world models, Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA), semantic communications, communication and control co-design, and learning-based approaches for networked intelligent systems.
