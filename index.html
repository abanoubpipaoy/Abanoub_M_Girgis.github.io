Postdoctoral Researcher at University of Vaasa · Aalto University · ELLIS Institute Finland

Bio. I am an ELLIS-funded Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Vaasa and Aalto University, Finland. My current research focuses on semantic communication and control co-design. I am supervised by Prof. Xiaoli Liu and Prof. Arno Solin . My PhD research, supervised by Prof. Mehdi Bennis , focused on communication and control co-design for beyond-5G networks, supervised and unsupervised learning, representation learning, and their applications in robotics and general decision-making. I also had the pleasure of visiting Nokia Bell Labs France and working with Dr. Alvaro Valcarce on AI and wireless research.

Interest. My current research aims to learn useful representations from robotics videos and connected vehicles for efficient prediction, communication, decision-making, and control.
